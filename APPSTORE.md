## Description

    ABYSM is a calm, free-swim dive from the sunlit surface all the way down to the bottom of the ocean - the 10,935 m Challenger Deep in the Mariana Trench.

    There are no goals, no timers, and no combat. Just you, a headlamp, and the slow weightless drift downward through five real ocean zones, meeting the strange and beautiful creatures that actually live at each depth.

    WHAT YOU GET
    - A continuous descent from the surface to the deepest point on Earth
    - 75 real sea creatures placed at their true depths, each with a one-line fact
    - 8 real ocean landmarks to find on the way down - the RMS Titanic, the USS Johnston, the Challenger Deep, and more
    - Five depth zones, from the warm Sunlight Zone to the crushing, lightless Hadal Zone
    - A headlamp that carves a glowing circle out of the darkening deep
    - Breach the surface like a dolphin, then sink back into the blue
    - Hand-made pixel art, procedurally generated ambient audio, and gentle haptics
    - Full game-controller support, or a simple on-screen joystick
    - Playable in 16 languages

    A QUIET KIND OF GAME
    ABYSM is meant to be played slowly. Put on headphones, turn the lights down, and just descend. The deeper you go, the darker, colder, and stranger it gets - until the anglerfish, the giant squid, and the things that have never seen the sun.

## Reviewer Notes

    ABYSM is a single-player, offline exploration game. There is no account, no login, and no network use.

    To test:
    1. Launch the app. Tap NEW GAME on the title screen (or CONTINUE to resume a saved dive).
    2. Use the bottom-left joystick to swim. The bottom-right buttons are, in a diamond: rise to surface (top), headlamp (bottom), boost (left), turbo (right).
    3. Swim straight down to descend through the zones; creatures and landmarks appear at their real depths.
    4. Top-left is Pause; top-right is the sound mute toggle.

    Game controllers are supported: left stick / d-pad to swim, A toggles the headlamp, B turbo, X boost, Y rise to surface, Menu pauses, L1 mutes.

    Data: the app stores only the current dive depth and horizontal position locally (UserDefaults) so it can offer "Continue." Nothing is collected, transmitted, or shared. Privacy nutrition label: Data Not Collected.

    The app is landscape-only and requires full screen.

    Note: pixel-art labels render in a retro bitmap font for Latin-script languages; languages in other scripts (Chinese, Japanese, Korean, Russian, Hindi, Arabic) render in a system font so every glyph displays correctly.
