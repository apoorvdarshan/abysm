# ABYSM Code of Conduct

## Our community

ABYSM welcomes people of all backgrounds and experience levels. Help make the
project a respectful place to ask questions, report problems, share ideas, and
contribute, regardless of age, disability, ethnicity, gender identity or
expression, nationality, race, religion, sexual orientation, or technical
experience.

This code applies to the repository, issues, pull requests, and anywhere
someone represents the project.

## Expected behavior

- Be considerate and patient, especially with people who are learning.
- Give specific, constructive feedback about the work, and listen to other views.
- Respect people's boundaries, privacy, and choices about what they share.
- Keep discussions on topic and use the right channel (see
  [CONTRIBUTING.md](CONTRIBUTING.md)).
- Accept corrections, own your mistakes, and help settle disagreements calmly.

## Unacceptable behavior

- Harassment, discrimination, threats, intimidation, or personal attacks.
- Sexual harassment, unwanted sexual attention, or sexualized content aimed at
  others.
- Publishing someone's private information, messages, or credentials without
  permission.
- Spam, scams, impersonation, malicious links, or deliberately disrupting
  discussions.
- Repeated unwanted contact, evading moderation, or retaliating against someone
  who reported a concern.

## Reporting a concern

Email **[ad13dtu@gmail.com](mailto:ad13dtu@gmail.com?subject=ABYSM%20conduct%20report)**
privately with the subject "ABYSM conduct report". Include what happened, links
to the relevant issues or comments, and screenshots if you have them. Do not
post sensitive evidence in a public issue.

Apoorv Darshan, the project maintainer, reviews conduct reports. Reports and
personal information are handled discreetly and shared only as needed to
address the concern. Retaliation is not permitted.

Security vulnerabilities follow the private process in [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md).

## Moderation

The maintainer may remove content, ask for a correction, give a warning, or
temporarily or permanently restrict participation. Responses depend on the
severity, the context, and whether the behavior is repeated. Serious misconduct
can lead straight to a restriction.

Anyone affected by a moderation decision may ask for it to be reviewed by
writing to the maintainer privately. These expectations apply to the maintainer
as well as to contributors and community members.
